Selected work

Computational neuroscience, independent project, since October 2026

Neuro Workbench

Working prototype; scientific validation in progress

A desktop lab for computational neuroscience that ties every number on screen to a recorded run. The current demonstration links a real 3D reconstruction of a hippocampal neuron to stored voltage traces from a passive cable model, checks them across two simulators, and says plainly what is simulated.
